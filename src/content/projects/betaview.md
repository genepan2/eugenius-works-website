---
title: BetaView
status: prototype
order: 7
description: >-
  Climbing routes made visible for the colorblind. With red-green color blindness it is often almost impossible to pick out the holds of one route in a bouldering gym. Tap one hold on a photo of the wall, and BetaView marks every hold of that route, in colors that colorblind people can tell apart clearly.
