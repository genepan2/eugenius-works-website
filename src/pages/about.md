# About

I am Eugen Panov. I design and build software, mostly small tools that solve a
problem I had first and someone else turned out to have too.

Eugenious Works is the Estonian company behind that work. It is a one-person
operation: the projects listed on this site, the occasional client engagement,
and the writing. There is no team, no funding, and no plan to acquire either.

My background is a mix of design and development, which in practice means I am
comfortable being the only person on a project and uncomfortable handing off a
half-specified one. Most of what I build is deliberately small. Most of what I
write is about why that is a reasonable way to work.

This site is where the projects and the writing live. It is plain on purpose —
working infrastructure first, visual identity later.
